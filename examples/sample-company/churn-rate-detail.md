# Churn-rate detail

**Summary:** This Level 2 node explains the evidence needed to interpret the `churn-rate` metric.

## Calculation

The calculation is customers lost during the period divided by eligible customers at the start. Verify
the period, population, exclusions, and reporting owner in the source before comparing values.
