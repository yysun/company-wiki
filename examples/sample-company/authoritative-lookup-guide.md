# Authoritative lookup guide

**Summary:** Use this guide when a question asks for the current official fact, owner, definition, or
policy.

## Reading path

1. Start at the relevant [People guide](people-guide.md) or [Customer guide](customer-guide.md).
2. Follow its `source of truth` or `defined by` link to the provider-native source document.
3. Check scope, status, effective date, owner, and conflicting historical or informal documents.
4. Answer with the source link and mark any interpretation or unresolved precedence.

## Source authority

The guide may say that an approved or canonical document is preferred, but the source must support its own
status. An informal note can reveal a missing question or a proposed alias; it cannot silently overrule a
current authoritative source.
