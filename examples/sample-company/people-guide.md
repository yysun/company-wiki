## Vacation policy

The `vacation-policy` explains how employees request and take time off. This statement is a guide-level
summary; the current HR document is the evidence to quote.

**Proposed aliases:** annual leave; time off. Confirm the mapping against the HR source before treating
the terms as interchangeable.
