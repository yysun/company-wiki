## Concepts

- **`active-customer`:** a customer with an active paid subscription on the evaluation date. Verify the
  definition and date in the product collection.
- **`customer-churn`:** a customer relationship ending during the measurement period. Do not assume this
  is the same as an account becoming inactive without reading the source definition.
- **`churn-rate`:** customers lost during the period divided by eligible customers at the start. Check
  numerator, denominator, period, and exclusions before interpreting movement.
