## Entitlement and requests

Eligible employees receive 18 paid annual leave days per calendar year. Annual leave is also called
vacation or time off in this policy. These terms do not include sick leave.

Requests for up to five consecutive working days require the line manager's approval. Requests for
six or more consecutive working days require both the line manager and a People Partner to approve.

## Carryover

Up to three unused days may be carried into the following year and must be used by March 31.
