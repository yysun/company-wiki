## Active customer

An active customer has an active paid subscription on the evaluation date. Trial-only and internal
test accounts are excluded. An account is not inactive merely because its users have not logged in.

## Customer churn

A customer churns when its last paid subscription ends during the measurement period. A cancellation
request is not churn until the subscription ends. A customer with another active paid subscription
has not churned.
