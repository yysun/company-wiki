# Maintain durable wiki knowledge

Maintain corrects or restructures a selected scope: merge or rename nodes, repair hierarchy/links/relationships,
refresh summaries, replace obsolete sources, archive branches, retire low-value nodes, and manage personal
controls. Preserve user-authored organization unless the governing user approves restructuring. Add Source—not
Maintain—reconciles new selected evidence.

Every correction has a concrete plan before it is applied. An explicit request to fix or update the selected wiki
authorizes the necessary bounded edits; a factual correction alone does not. State scope, affected pages,
evidence, conflicts, exact edits, and preserved organization, then use the
[durable change protocol](change-protocol.md). Honor review-first instructions and ask when restructuring needs
a decision beyond the user's request. A removal unlinks the node
and rewrites it as a `Retired` stub; a merge preserves a `Merged into` stub. Do not delete provider pages.
For disclosure cleanup, the [publication boundary](publication.md) overrides ordinary stub preservation: no restricted
title, backlink, alias, or relationship may remain in a broader page. Propose exact destination-safe replacements,
use version protection, and report unresolved native history/search exposure. Do not claim old copies were erased.
Freshness is `current`, `potentially stale`, `superseded`, or `needs review`; changing it needs evidence and user
authorization through the shared protocol.
