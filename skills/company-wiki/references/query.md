# Explore and Query

Both routes are read-only. Select one profile through [Registry](registry.md), then make one routing decision from
its compact context: Personal home as prior, named Company Index home as router, and at most three declared routing
pages per scope. Choose graph edges, likely source areas, and direct searches together before evidence retrieval.
Never turn home → guide → detail into sequential routing hops or return to routing after the first source read.

Retrieve inside registered accessible sources in bounded rounds. Search remains available when wiki coverage is
missing, stale, or unrelated. Bypass both wikis for exact identifiers, recent/unrepresented documents, a more
efficient direct search, an unavailable wiki, or an explicit request. Check metadata/size before reads and report
bound exhaustion and remaining uncertainty.

**Explore** reports transient routes and evidence and may offer Curate. **Query** retrieves the smallest useful
original evidence, cites every factual claim from evidence read in the same operation, labels interpretation and
conflicts, and writes nothing. A wiki statement without same-operation evidence is unverified navigation, not fact.
Recheck current source access/audience before exposing a derived route; fail closed on unavailable access.
