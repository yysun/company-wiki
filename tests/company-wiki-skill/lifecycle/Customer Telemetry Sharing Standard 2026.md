# Customer Telemetry Sharing Standard 2026

**Status:** Approved
**Effective date:** 2026-08-25
**Owner:** Data Governance

## Customer sharing rule

Robot telemetry may be shared with a customer only when an active data-processing agreement covers the
requested use and the shared data is aggregated to the minimum granularity needed for that use.

Raw device identifiers and telemetry belonging to other customers must not be included.

## Authority

This standard supersedes informal action items or planning notes about creating a telemetry-sharing policy.
Questions about an uncovered use case must be sent to Data Governance for a decision.
