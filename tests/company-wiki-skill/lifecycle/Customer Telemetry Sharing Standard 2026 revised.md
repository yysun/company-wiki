# Customer Telemetry Sharing Standard 2026

**Status:** Approved revised edition
**Effective date:** 2026-09-04
**Owner:** Data Governance

## Customer sharing rule

Robot telemetry may be shared with a customer only when an active data-processing agreement covers the
requested use, Data Governance has approved the request in writing, and the shared data is aggregated to the
minimum granularity needed for that use.

Raw device identifiers and telemetry belonging to other customers must not be included.

## Authority

This revision replaces the 2026-08-25 edition. The added written-approval condition is mandatory. Questions
about an uncovered use case must be sent to Data Governance for a decision.
